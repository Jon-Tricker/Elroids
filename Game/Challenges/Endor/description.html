Escape from Endor.

Objectives

  • Undock.
  • Make some money.
  • Fix up ship.
  • Get somewhere other than Endor.

Back Story

It was supposed to be an 'easy payday'. Use the last of your credits, and an investment from an unknown 'investor', to buy guns from the crooks on Asteel. Then sell them for a fat profit on Endor.

Then it all went wrong. Unusually high Raider and Saucer activity destroyed a significant part of the cargo and trashed the ship.

Finally, when you limped into Endor station, it turned out that half the crates were filled with scrap.

Now it turns out your 'investor' was backed the infamous Ewok crime lord 'Boss' Snuggles Fluffikins. Fluffikins is not happy and has 'invited' you for a 'meeting'.

As the bag is removed from your head the first thing you see is Fluffikins himself. You know he's dangerous. But he's 'just a Ted'. How bad can it be? Then you notice the five goons with plasma rifles ... It could be quite bad.

"Well?", purrs Fluffikins, "U' gona pay, pay? Or Snuggles gona put u' down for an iccle snoozy, nap?".

"Wait!" You say, rummaging in your pack. "I have something that may interest you." This is a million to one shot. But, hey, you're dead anyway. "It's, it's ... a Thermal Detonator!"

The room turns violet. Five seconds latter you are staggering out of a cloud of ozone and burned fur. Patting out the remaining small fires in your clothing you sprint for the space dock. A few, badly aimed, plasma bolts follow you down the corridor.

At least, assuming Fluffikins knew his work, the security cameras were probably disabled. So there should be no hassle from the police.

There is you ship. She's beaten up, there has been no time, or credits, for repairs, but it will have to do.

First thing, get undocked. Then make some money (mining or trading) and patch up the ship. 'Teds' don't understand much about technology. So you will have to do most of the work yourself.

Then to get out of this dump to a higher tech system where proper repairs could be done.
