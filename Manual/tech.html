Guide to technology levels.

Each system has a technology level with 1 being the lowest. These roughly correspond to:

  • 1 - No/'Stone age' technology
  • 2 - Metal usage.
  • 3 - Industrial. Steam, diesel and steel.
  • 4 - Early 'space age'. Orbital rockets, nuclear power and computers.
  • 5 - Inter planetary travel, AI and robots.
  • 6 - Basic inter stellar tarvel.
  • 7 - Routine inter stellar tarvel.
  • 8 - Inter galactic.
  • 9 - Time travel.
