Guide to weapons and combat.

Weapons

Weapons are a class of 'component' that can be mounted to 'ships'.

When fired each weapon type produces a specific 'projectile'. Projectiles fall into two catagories.

  • Missiles - Relativly slow but have a long range.
  • Beams - Fast but short range.

Weapons may malfunction if status is < 50%.

For now all projectiles are unguided.

Projectile are emmited from a 'gun position'. Currently on the underside of the ships bow.
